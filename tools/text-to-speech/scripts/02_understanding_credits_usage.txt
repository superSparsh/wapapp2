In this video, we will understand Credits Used on the Dashboard so you always know your messaging capacity.

Open the Dashboard and look at the Credits Used section. You will see four cards: Sent, Marketing Conversations, Utility Conversations, and Service messages.

Sent shows how many messages went out in the selected period against your limit. Marketing and Utility track conversation categories that affect WhatsApp billing. Service messages cover support-style traffic, including free allowance when your account shows it.

Use Sort by to switch between Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Yearly, or All time. Watch the cards update so you can compare busy days with quieter periods.

Tap Refresh after a campaign or inbox activity to pull the newest counts. If you see free service remaining, remember free messages are counted per WhatsApp number and are not shared across numbers.

Check these cards before large sends. If you are close to a limit, pause and plan the next batch carefully.
