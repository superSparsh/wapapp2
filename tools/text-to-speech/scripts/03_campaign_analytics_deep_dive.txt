This video is a deep dive into Campaign Analytics on the Dashboard — reviewing recently sent campaigns in detail.

Scroll to Review Your Recently Sent Campaigns. Open the dropdown and select the campaign you want to inspect. If nothing is listed yet, send a test campaign first, then return here.

The recipient table shows name, WhatsApp number, campaign name, sent time, status, and a reason when delivery failed. Scroll through the rows to spot patterns such as repeated failures on one number or template.

Below the table, metric cards summarize Total Recipients, Delivered, Failed, Read, Response, and Unsubscribed, each with a percentage. High failed or unsubscribe rates are early warning signs.

Use Refresh so the table and cards stay in sync after new deliveries. Tap More for full campaign statistics, or open a metric card for a filtered recipient list.

Make this review part of every send — confirm delivery health before you launch the next campaign.
