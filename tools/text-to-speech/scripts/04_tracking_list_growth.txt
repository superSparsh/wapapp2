This video shows how to track List Growth from the Dashboard and keep your audience healthy.

Scroll to the List Growth section near the bottom of the Dashboard. You will see cards for average subscribe rate, average unsubscribe rate, total unsubscribers, and total blacklisted contacts.

Subscribe rate tells you how well new contacts are joining. Unsubscribe rate and total unsubscribers show who is leaving. Blacklisted contacts are numbers you should not message again.

Below the cards, open the Subscribers chart. It shows how subscriptions move over time — useful after a campaign, import, or promo when you expect a spike.

Review List Growth at least weekly. Rising unsubscribes or blacklist counts may mean targeting is too broad or messages are too frequent.

If a campaign just finished, refresh the Dashboard first, then compare List Growth with campaign response metrics. Together they show whether people engaged and whether they stayed subscribed.

Use these insights before building the next audience list so growth stays steady and quality stays high.
