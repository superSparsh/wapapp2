Welcome to Form Builder. This is where you create and manage signup forms for lead generation on WhatsApp.

From the left sidebar, open Form builder. You land on the Form Builder dashboard. Search forms by name, sort by Newest first, Oldest first, Name A to Z, Name Z to A, or Status, then tap Refresh to reload.

The table shows Form Name, Form URL, Communication Status with Sent, Read, Delivered, and Failed counts, Status as Active or Inactive, En/Disable, and Actions. Use Edit to open the builder. Under the more menu you can open Statistics, Copy Link, or Delete a form. Keep only current campaign forms Active so old links stop collecting leads.

If the list is empty, you will see Create Your First Form. Otherwise tap Create Form to start a new signup form. Use this dashboard to find live forms, copy their public URL, and turn forms on or off without opening the editor. Next we create your first form.
