This video shows how to add a new contact so you can start a WhatsApp conversation from Inbox.

On the Inbox toolbar, tap Add New Contact. A modal opens asking for the details you need.

Enter the contact Name. Choose the Country Code, then type the Phone Number carefully with the correct digits. Pick Response Type — Human Response if your team will reply, or AI Response if the bot should handle the chat.

Tap Save Contact. WapApp creates the contact and opens the conversation in your list. If that number already exists, you will see an error so you do not create a duplicate.

New contacts often start outside the twenty-four hour window. Send an approved template or opt-in first. When the customer replies, you can continue with normal messages.

Use Add New Contact whenever someone reaches you offline — phone, form, or store — and you want the thread ready in Inbox.
