In this video, you will learn how to export chat history from Inbox.

There are two export paths. First, open a conversation and tap Export Chat in the green header. That downloads a CSV for the open chat — useful when you need one customer’s full thread.

Second, use Export by date on the Inbox toolbar. A modal asks for the date range. Pick a quick preset such as Today, Last seven days, Last thirty days, or This month, or set From and To dates yourself.

Choose what to export: all chats in that date range, or only the open chat if one is selected. Optionally skip specific phone numbers by pasting them one per line.

Tap Download CSV. Open the file in Excel or Google Sheets for support reviews, audits, or offline follow-up.

Export regularly for important accounts, and always respect privacy rules for your team. That is how you export chat history from Inbox.
