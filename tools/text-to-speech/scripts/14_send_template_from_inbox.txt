This video shows how to send a WhatsApp template from Inbox — essential when the twenty-four hour session has expired.

Open the conversation you want to reach. If the amber session banner appears, free-form messages are blocked until the customer replies again.

Tap Send Template Message under the composer, or open the plus menu and choose Send Templates. The Send Templates modal opens with a list of approved templates for the active WhatsApp line.

Select a template. If it has variables, fill each parameter carefully so the preview looks correct. The phone preview on the side shows how the message will appear to the customer.

Tap Send Template. Only templates approved on WhatsApp with a valid template code can be sent. If the list is empty, sync templates from the Templates page first.

Sending a template does not by itself open the free-form window. The customer still needs to reply once. After they reply, you can continue with normal messages, media, and other session actions.

Use templates for re-engagement, reminders, and first outreach from Inbox.
