In this video, you will share products and request payment directly from an Inbox conversation.

First, open a chat where the session allows interactive messages. Tap the plus icon, then open Buttons and Lists. From there you can choose Share One Product or Share Many Products.

For one product, enter the catalog ID and product retailer ID, add an optional body message, and send. For many products, add a header, body, catalog ID, section title, and up to ten retailer IDs — one SKU per line — then tap Send Products.

To collect money, open Request Payment from the plus menu, or tap Send Payment Link under the composer when session actions are shown. Enter the Amount and a clear Description such as an order number.

Tap Send Payment Link. WapApp creates a Razorpay payment link and sends it on WhatsApp using your Commerce payment template. The customer can pay without leaving WhatsApp.

Use products to help customers browse, and payment links to close the sale in the same chat. Keep catalog IDs and amounts accurate before you send.
