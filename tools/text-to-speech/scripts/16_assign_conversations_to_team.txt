This video shows how to assign Inbox conversations to your team so the right person replies every time.

Open a conversation. In the green chat header, find the Assign agent dropdown. Choose Unassigned, or pick a teammate from the list of assignable agents.

After you assign, the conversation list can show the assignee name on the thread row. That helps everyone see who owns the chat without opening it.

To focus your own queue, open Filters and set Assignee to yourself, another teammate, or only unassigned chats. Combine this with Scope and Date range when the inbox is busy.

Team members typically see chats assigned to them and lines they are allowed to access. Owners and admins can reassign when workload needs balancing.

Assign new leads quickly, reassign when someone is away, and keep Unassigned low so no customer waits without an owner. That is conversation assignment in Inbox — clear ownership, faster replies, happier customers.
