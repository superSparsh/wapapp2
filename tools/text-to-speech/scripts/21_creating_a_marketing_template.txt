In this video, you will create a Marketing template — the standard path for promotional WhatsApp messages that need Meta approval.

Open Templates, stay on Regular Templates, and tap Create New Template. The builder opens with Template Name, Category, and Language fields at the top of every step.

Enter a clear template name using only lowercase letters, numbers, and underscores — for example summer_sale_offer. Spaces and capitals are not allowed. Choose Category Marketing, then pick Language such as English UK or English US.

You land on the Body step first. Write your promotional message, add variables if needed, and watch the phone preview update on the side. Continue through Header, Footer, and Buttons in the following videos, then submit for WhatsApp approval.

Marketing templates are for offers, announcements, and re-engagement. Keep the tone clear and compliant so Meta is more likely to approve them quickly.
