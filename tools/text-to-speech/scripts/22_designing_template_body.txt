This video covers Designing the Template Body — the main text customers read in your WhatsApp template.

In the builder, open the Body step. The body field is required and supports up to one thousand twenty-four characters. Type your message, or use Bold, Italic, Strike, and emoji from the toolbar under the editor.

Tap plus Variable to insert placeholders such as customer name. Sample values appear below so the phone preview looks realistic before you send. If AI suggestions are enabled on your account, use AI for draft ideas, then edit carefully before you save.

For Utility templates you may also see quick presets that fill a starter body. For Authentication, the body is a fixed OTP line and is read-only — we cover that in a later video.

Check the live phone preview as you write. When the body looks right, tap Next to continue to Header or the next step for your category.

A strong body is short, clear, and uses variables only where personalization truly matters.
