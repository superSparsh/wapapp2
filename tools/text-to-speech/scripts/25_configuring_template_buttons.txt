In this video, you will configure Template Buttons so customers can take action in one tap without typing a reply.

Open the Buttons step and choose a Button Group: None, Call To Action, Quick Reply, WhatsApp Flows, or Mixed for CTA plus Quick Reply together.

For Call To Action, add Website, Call, or Unsubscribe style actions — respect limits such as maximum URL buttons and phone buttons shown under the selector. For Quick Reply, add short reply labels customers can tap. For WhatsApp Flows, select a flow you already created in Flow Builder.

Button text has a short character limit, so keep labels clear and action-oriented. Watch the phone preview as you add each button so the layout stays tidy.

Choose None if the template is text only. Save and continue to Final Review when the actions match your goal — visit a site, call support, start a flow, or reply quickly.
