This video covers the Limited Time Offer Body — the main promotional text under the offer strip.

With Category Limited Time Offer selected, open the Body step and write the main message. Explain what the customer gets, any conditions in plain language, and a clear call to act before the offer ends. Add variables if you personalize the message.

Use the toolbar for formatting and plus Variable for placeholders. Check the phone preview often — you will also see the LTO offer strip once discount introduction and related settings are configured on the LTO step.

Do not put the entire coupon story only in the body if you plan to use the copy-code button and discount introduction. Keep the body focused on value and urgency, and let LTO settings handle the short offer line and code.

Save and continue. Header, LTO settings, and buttons come next in the flow. Take your time on this screen during recording so viewers can follow each click clearly.
