This video shows Limited Time Offer Buttons — the actions customers tap after they see your deal.

Open the Buttons step. For LTO templates you can use standard button groups, and you will also see Limited Time Offer Copy Code as a dedicated button option for coupons.

Add a copy-code style action so customers can copy the coupon in one tap. You may also add a Website button to send them to the landing page or cart. Keep button labels short and within the character limit shown in the builder.

If you set a coupon on the LTO settings step, it can feed the copy-code button automatically. Confirm the phone preview shows both the offer strip and the buttons correctly before you leave this step.

Save when actions match your campaign goal — copy the code, visit the site, or both. Take your time on this screen during recording so viewers can follow each click clearly.
