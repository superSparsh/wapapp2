This video covers Carousel Template Design — building the swipeable cards themselves.

Open the Carousel step. Review or edit the carousel intro, then build each card. Add between the minimum and maximum card count shown on screen — typically two to ten cards.

Each card needs media such as an image or video upload, a short card body within the character limit, and one or two buttons. Button types and order must match across all cards or WhatsApp will reject the template.

Use Add card for more items, and open Guidelines if you need a reminder of carousel rules. Check the preview as you go so media quality and button labels stay consistent from card to card.

When every card is complete and matching, save and continue to the final submit step. Take your time on this screen during recording so viewers can follow each click clearly.
