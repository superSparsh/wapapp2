This video covers Activating and Managing Your Automation.

On the design page, use Toggle automation in the header to turn the journey on or off. On the Drip Marketing list, the En/Disable control and Status column show Running or Paused. Only enable after Save Flow and Settings dates look correct.

Use Copy on the list to duplicate an automation when you need a similar journey with small changes. Use Edit to reopen design. Delete removes an automation after you confirm Delete automation — this cannot be undone, so export or copy first if unsure. After enabling, send a test contact through the path before a large list starts entering.

In Settings, update Automation name, trigger, Audience, timezone, Start Date, and End Date, then Save. Keep names clear so Running automations are easy to audit. Management is ongoing: pause when offers end, and re-enable when a new campaign period starts. Next we review the Insight tab.
