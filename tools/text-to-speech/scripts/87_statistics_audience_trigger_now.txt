This video covers the Statistics Tab, Audience View, and Trigger Now.

Open Statistics to see Entered, Completed, Dropped, and Errors. Use Refresh, View History, and Export to CSV when you need a report. Automation Performance summarizes totals, Completion Rate, and Top Nodes by Activity so you know which steps are busiest.

Switch to the Audience tab for Contacts in action, Contacts done, Skipped or Pending, and Error. Search and filter with All Contacts as needed. For a contact Waiting in a delay, you may see Trigger Now. Confirm with Are you sure, then Confirm — this manually advances that contact and should be used carefully for testing or support. Export CSV reports after major campaigns so you can compare completion rates over time.

Open the Timeline sub-tab for activity history such as Entered, Completed, Dropped, and Error events. Together, Statistics and Audience tell you volume and individual progress. Next we build a Specific Date trigger automation.
